## 2. Cross-page patterns

**Typography system**
- One family everywhere, RAG-Sans-C. Weights used: **ExtraBold** (headlines, offers), **SemiBold** (date lines, sticky messages, labels, card titles), **Regular** (subs, CTA labels, legal). There is no Light or Black. The only exception is the Apple tagline in SF Pro Medium.
- **Two weights in one text node** is the standard 2-line pattern: line 1 heavy, line 2 Regular ("היי דליה,⏎מה שלומך היום?"; "רוצים לשמוע עוד?⏎מלאו פרטים..."; "תפעול משיבון...⏎כל מה שחשוב..."). Sometimes the order is reversed, with the Regular eyebrow on top and the ExtraBold headline below (iPhone Duo phase 2).
- Ratios measured:
  - Headline : sub = 1 : 0.64–0.87. Cases: voicemail 28/18 = 0.64; form 24/20 = 0.83; iPhone 47.3/41 = 0.87; NY desktop 107.5/28 = 0.26 for display-size headlines.
  - Eyebrow : headline = 1 : 1.8 (NY 59.7/107.5) or 0.87 : 1 (Duo 33/38).
  - CTA label is **always Regular 20** on desktop (19.3 on NY mobile).
  - Legal is 14 desktop / 11–12 mobile, so about 0.3× a 47px headline.
  - Date line ≈ headline size (49.7 vs 47.3) in iPhone 18 phase 1.
- Desktop → mobile scaling: iPhone headline 47.3 → 32.4–33 (×0.69); NY 107.5 → 52.5 (×0.49) and sub 28 → 18; popup is exactly ×0.51 (38 → 19.4); greeting 40 → 24.
- Colors: black `#000` on light backgrounds, white on dark. Accent text is purple `#810ea9` (selected states, links, required asterisks, text on white CTAs) or yellow `#f3fe7e` / `#f4fe7e` (offer lines on dark purple, highlight box). The NY holiday banner uses gold gradient type. Gray `#757475` is for secondary labels and placeholders.

**Text block position and width**
- Desktop 1920 promos: the text column sits **right of center**, never at the far right edge:
  - iPhone 18 text x 1082–1548 (center ≈1315)
  - Duo x 1255–1643
  - NY x 1115–1591 (476 wide, right-aligned)
  - Column width is about 340–500px (18–26% of 1920). The image is placed left-center, roughly x 358–1110. This matches the RTL reading order: text first (right), image second (left).
- Text alignment: centered on the light Apple canvases and on all mobiles. Right-aligned on the NY desktop dark panel and on card titles.
- Mobile 430x480 / 375x466: text is always **centered at the top** (y ≈ 27–190), CTA under it, and the product or photo fills the bottom 45–55%, usually bleeding off the bottom edge.

**Offers and device launches**
- In this group the iPhone banners carry **no price, monthly price, payments or trade-in**. They only state the phase ("פתיחת ההזמנה המוקדמת", "ההזמנה המוקדמת החלה!", "המכירה החלה!"), a date and time, and a benefit ("מזמינים היום ומקבלים לפני כולם", "משלוח חינם עד הבית").
- Date format: "15:00 | 12.9.26", meaning time, a pipe with spaces, then D.M.YY. Mobile Duo order: "הזמנה מוקדמת | 16.10.26 | 15:00".
- Other prices: "ב-9.90₪ בחודש" (₪ glued to the number, "ב-" prefix, SemiBold, same size as the title) and "20% הנחה בחשבון" (yellow ExtraBold).

**Accent colors besides purple**
- Yellow-lime `#f3fe7e` / `#f4fe7e` for the discount offer and highlight boxes (Energy).
- Gold gradient for the holiday headline.
- Magenta/pink glows `#cd62a2`, `#834a98`, `#cc75d7`.
- iPhone campaigns use none of these: only `#f5f5f7`, black text and the purple CTA.

**Decorative devices**
- Neon light rings or ribbons orbiting the hero object: around the 5G phone, around the NY TV, the light-wave behind the Energy family, light streaks in WiFi.
- Podium / floor reflection: 5G phone on a white disc, WiFi reflective floor.
- Blurred glow ellipses (NY, voicemail opt 1).
- Convex curved panel edge (NY desktop), convex white arc (personal area), wavy shape (voicemail opt 3).
- Dark linear gradient bands behind text for readability (popup, voicemail, WiFi, drama).
- Pill badges ("הנמכר ביותר").
- Apple campaigns: no decoration at all.

**Separating image from text**
- (a) Flat-colored "safe zone": the image fades into a flat dark color where the text goes (drama `#0e0023`, WiFi `#1f0257`, voicemail `#3d2a4b`).
- (b) A hard shaped panel (NY curve, popup split 373/427).
- (c) A top gradient band on mobile.
- (d) On `#f5f5f7` Apple canvases, whitespace alone does the job.

**CTAs**
- Pill shape. Desktop 240x50 r33 (hero), sticky 108–156x44 r41, mobile 171–178x39–40.
- Fill: the purple gradient `#810ea9→#bc32ce`, or solid `#810ea9` in the DS Button/h50/primary (popup). On dark creatives, a **white pill with purple text** (NY).
- Labels are short imperatives in plural: "הירשמו עכשיו", "הזמינו עכשיו", "לרכישה", "אשמח שתחזורו אלי" (first person, for lead forms), and the text link "לפרטים נוספים ורכישה".
- CTA sits under the text block, aligned with it (centered or right).

**Copy phrasing (Hebrew)**
- Second-person plural verbs: "מצטרפים ונהנים", "מזמינים היום ומקבלים", "מלאו פרטים ונחזור אליכם".
- Exclamation marks on milestones ("החלה!", "בשירות!").
- Benefit plus qualifier ("ללא עלות נוספת", "לפני כולם", "עד הבית").
- Holiday wordplay ("מתחילים שנה חדשה בראש שקט", a pun on Rosh Hashanah and "peace of mind").
- Personal greeting with the first name ("היי דליה,").
- Superlatives ("הטוב בישראל", "מספר 1").

**Line breaks**
- Headlines break into 2 balanced short lines, each about 2–4 words.
- The popup builds each line as its own text frame.
- Offers are split so that the number lands on its own line ("מצטרפים ונהנים מעד" / "20% הנחה בחשבון").
- Mobile subs keep the desktop line breaks.

**Legal text**
- Seen only in the lead popup: centered, between the fields and the CTA, 14/11.3px, black, with a purple inline link.
- None on the promo banners in this group.

**Article (כתבה) banners vs promo banners**
- Article sets (5G PRO, WiFi) are **text-free illustrations**: one hero subject (glowing device or glyph) in neon purple. They come as 1920x335 + 1500x1560 + 314x160 + 273x160, with large empty dark-violet sky or side areas reserved for text.
- Promo banners have live text and a CTA, in 1920x440/500 + 430x480 + stickies.

**Holiday tone**
- Rosh Hashanah is warm and luxurious: a real festive table, gold type, a dark plum panel with pink glows, and a white CTA.
- There is no memorial content on these pages.

## 4. Inconsistencies and surprises
- CTA fill varies: the gradient `#810ea9→#bc32ce` on campaigns vs solid `#810ea9` on the DS button in the popup. Radius varies too: 25, 28, 33 and 41 for similar pills.
- Two near-identical yellows: `#f3fe7e` (popup) and `#f4fe7e` (Energy box). Two purples: `#810ea9` (CTA/brand) and `#9631ff` (default frame fills, Energy headline text).
- Fractional font sizes everywhere (47.305, 38.026, 19.361, 32.432). They come from scaling layers instead of fixed type tokens.
- iPhone Duo desktop 1909:495 contains a **duplicated** background plus logo plus "Hello, hello." pair, offset by 38px. Duo sticky 1855:42 contains an image layer still named "WWEN_iPhone_18_Pro…" (copied from iPhone 18).
- The drama channel frames are named "לובי 5g - טלפונים עומדים" (copied names). The WiFi 314x160 thumbnail hides a whole off-canvas Energy article banner.
- Voicemail text box is 434 wide inside a 375 frame (x −29). It is centered, so it renders fine, but it overflows.
- The iPhone 18 phase 1 date line (SemiBold 49.7) is **larger** than its ExtraBold headline (47.3). On Duo the date is smaller (Regular 32 vs 35).
- Mobile personal area mixes RAG-Sans-N (the "חשמל" item) into RAG-Sans-C.
- No prices or trade-in in the iPhone launch banners. If the agent needs device pricing typesetting, look at other groups.
