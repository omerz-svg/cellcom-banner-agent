## 2. Cross-page patterns

**Text block position and width**
- **Desktop promo (1920x480/500):** the text column is right-aligned and its right edge sits ~330px in from the frame edge (1590 on 1920), not at the edge. Column width is 460–564px (24–29% of the width).
  - 5G is the exception: headline right edge at 1505 (415 in), with the price block on the opposite (left-center) side of the product at x≈354–646.
- **Mobile promo (430x480, 375x466):** text sits stacked in the top 45–55%, image below.
  - Triple and Yom Kippur center the text.
  - 5G mobile right-aligns it, 20px from the right edge.
- **Article square (1500x1560):** the title and sub are centered, the title starts at y≈150 (~10% from top), the sub at y≈288, and the text block occupies the top ~26–33%.
- **Article strip (1920x335):** text right-aligned in a column x=708–1475 (767 wide, right margin 445), title at y≈49, sub at y≈188.
- **Strip (1300x100):** right-aligned with a tight 15px margin, a single line nearly 900px wide.

**Size ratios**
- Article: sub = 0.60 × title (71.8/119.3) on every SemiBold version; strip 34–39.6/60.6 = 0.56–0.65. Title line-height is 88%.
- Triple: eyebrow 0.77 × headline (40/52). Price numeral = 3.1 × headline (161.5/52). Currency ₪ = 0.41 × numeral. "רק/ב-" = 0.28 × numeral. CTA label 20 = 0.38 × headline.
- 5G: lead-in 0.64 × headline (53/82.7). Numeral = 1.83 × headline (151/82.7). Decimals = 0.30 × numeral. ₪ = 0.53 × numeral. Terms 24 = 0.16 × numeral.
- **Mobile scale vs desktop:** 0.58–0.66 for promo banners (Triple 0.64–0.66, 5G 0.56–0.63, Yom Kippur 0.50–0.56). The CTA label barely shrinks (20 → 19.3).

**How prices are typeset**
- The integer is a huge ExtraBold numeral.
- The ₪ is about half its size and sits to the left of the number (the visual end in RTL).
- The prefix ("החל מ-" / "רק⏎ב-") sits to the right at about 0.2–0.3×, stacked or baseline-aligned.
- Decimals ("90") appear as a small superscript at top-left of the integer.
- Duration or terms ("למשך חודשיים", "לחודש למנוי למשך חודשיים") go below in Regular.
- The price is either gold-gradient (Triple) or white with a glow (5G). Prices are never purple.

**Accent colors beyond purple**
- The gold gradient is the only secondary accent, used for:
  - the Yom Kippur title (holiday, festive dignity)
  - the Triple eyebrow, numeral and ₪
  - the 5G terms-pill stroke
- No orange or other accent colors appear. The orange in the Triple banner comes from the autumn photography only.

**Decorative devices**
- Organic curved wave panels: magenta-to-purple `#bc32ce/#810ea9` gradient or dark purple. They act as the text panel in 1650:1161, 1826:205, 1826:221 and the Triple banners.
- White elliptical lobby wave at the bottom (5G opt 2).
- Neon light ribbons and rings around products (5G, sports, globe).
- Blurred glow ellipses (r85–101) behind text.
- Top fades of 100% → 70% → 0% of `#e9def5` (light) or `#160229` (dark) to make a text zone over photos.
- Glowing icon bubbles (shield / 5G / SIM / check) for cellular features.
- The props are recurring Cellcom products: white router with the star logo, mesh unit, purple remote, purple lamps and pillows. Purple is planted in the scene itself.

**Image/text separation**
1. A side split with a curved purple panel (Triple, moving strip).
2. A fade overlay on the same photo, with text over a calm sky or wall zone (articles).
3. The product centered with copy split on both sides (5G).
4. A blurred black mask over the photo (memorial strip).

There are no hard rectangular boxes behind text.

**CTA**
- Only the Triple banners have one: "לרכישה", a white pill (r≈28–33, height 39–50) with a Regular-weight purple label `#810ea9`.
- 5G and holiday banners have no CTA. Articles never have a CTA.

**Copy patterns (Hebrew)**
- Paired rhythmic slogans: "הכי משתלם. הכי מהיר. הכי 5G", "הערבים מתקררים, הבינג' מתחמם".
- Article titles are a question hook plus a guide promise: "טסים עם הילדים?" + "המדריך המלא ל…"; "עוברים דירה?" + "כך תדאגו ש…".
- Plural second person throughout (תדאגו, אתכם, מצטרפים).
- Articles use a numbered-list hook: "4 שדרוגים…".

**Line breaks**
- Subs are broken manually into 2 balanced lines with ⏎. The break comes after a natural phrase ("…חבילות⏎גלישה…", "…התקשורת⏎יעברו אתכם בקלות").
- Article titles run 1 line, or 2 when there is a year ("לעונת 2026/2027").
- Mobile promo headlines may break to 2 lines (Triple opt 1).
- The memorial strip breaks into 2 lines on mobile only.

**Legal**
- No legal or disclaimer line appears in any banner in this group. The only fine print is the terms pill in 5G.

**Article (כתבה) format vs promo**
- **Article deliverable set:** 1500x1560 square, 1920x335 strip, 314x160 and 273x160 thumbnails.
- Final sets are mostly image-only, with text left to HTML.
- **Where article text is baked in:**
  - Title: 1–4 words, often a question, SemiBold 119.3.
  - Sub: 6–10 words, Regular 71.8.
  - Centered on the square, right-aligned on the strip.
- **What articles lack:** no CTA, no price, no logo.
- Promo banners use ExtraBold headlines, prices and CTAs, at 1920x480/500 and mobile 375–430 wide.

**Memorial / holiday restraint**
- **Memorial (יום הזיכרון):**
  - Pure black background, white ExtraBold text, a single symbolic photo (flag + candle).
  - No brand purple, no gold, no CTA, no logo, no offer.
  - One factual sentence naming the event.
- **Yom Kippur:**
  - Keeps the brand purple atmosphere and allows the gold gradient on the title.
  - A single symbolic object (shofar) and the traditional greeting "גמר חתימה טובה".
  - No commercial message or CTA.
