# Cellcom Campaigns file, group B: visual and typographic study

File `zyp67jdVgIYSgUoyJD8XzM` ("Campaigns"). Read-only study: screenshots of every listed section, plus layer reads of representative frames.
Font everywhere: **RAG-Sans-Cellcom** (Regular / SemiBold / ExtraBold). The only other font is Samsung's brand font (Bold) for the product name "Galaxy Z Fold8".
Coordinates are x,y,w,h in px, relative to the banner frame. Hebrew text is RTL and right-aligned on desktop.

## 2. Cross-page patterns

**Formats seen**
- Desktop: 1920x335 (lobby/product hero, the most common) and 1920x500 (homepage).
- Mobile: 430x480 (homepage pairs) and 390x390 (product/lobby heroes).
- Others: 1440x360, 632x400 double banners, 358x400 cards, 300x410 and 260x310 tiles.

**Text block position (desktop)**
- Always on the right side (RTL), right-aligned.
- Right edge at x=1600 (Energy, HOMEPAGE: exactly 320 px inset), 1591 (Fold), 1564 (upgrades), 1476/1442 (Elbit), 1475 (article).
- This matches the moving-campaign spec: 320 px side bands are cropped below 1280 px, and the recommended max text box is 620 px wide.
- Actual widths vary from 556 to 864. Headline boxes usually start between y≈38 and 130 of 335.
- Exception: on the Russian page the text is LTR, left-aligned, starting at x=1017 inside the right half.

**Text block position (mobile)**
- Text is always centered and stacked at the top. Top padding is about 17–68 px, and the stack takes the top ~35–45%.
- The image occupies the bottom half. It is usually re-composed and centered, not just cropped from desktop.

**Size ratios**
- Eyebrow is 0.31–0.54 of the headline on desktop and 0.5–0.78 on mobile, so mobile flattens the hierarchy.
- Sub is about 0.5–0.75 of the headline: Fold 0.5, article 0.65, Elbit 0.70, Elbit inner 0.75, upgrades 0.68.
- CTA label is 20 px, fixed, on both 1920x500 desktops.
- Legal is 18 desktop and 16 mobile.
- Desktop headlines: 56–80 typical, 128 for a number-led headline. Mobile headlines: 24–44.
- Line height is about 1.0 for multi-line headlines.

**Weights**
- The headline is ExtraBold, often mixed with Regular inside the same box. Examples: "הצטרפו" Regular + brand/benefit ExtraBold; Energy line 3 Regular.
- The sub is Regular or SemiBold. Eyebrows are always Regular.

**Prices and numbers**
- Big numerals use the brand gradient or a larger size inside the sentence:
  - "1Gb"/"₪20" at 50.3 inside a 40.2 headline.
  - "30 ימי ניסיון" at 128.
- AppRU price lockup: the integer "49" at about 2× the unit text, the decimals ".90" at 0.32× the integer and bottom-aligned, then the unit "шек. в месяц" in black ExtraBold.
- The ₪ sign comes before the number in Hebrew copy ("₪20").
- Moving campaign: a 3D extruded "200₪" render instead of live type.

**Accent colors besides purple**
- Energy yellow #f3fe7e: only in Cellcom Energy banners, for the headline or the brand phrase.
- Elbit gold gradient (#c99d3d→#fff1b7→#c99d3d): only in Elbit headlines.
- Pink glow ellipses #f6b7bd / #ff82a6: behind the wave seams (article, AppRU mobile).
- No orange anywhere.

**Core purple palette**
- #810ea9 and #bc32ce, used as a linear gradient pair everywhere: panels, CTA pills, gradient text.
- #9631ff appears as a frame background.
- Dark grounds: #1a0030, #010011, #020212, #03030e.

**Decorative devices**
- Curved wave / convex edge separating the photo from the purple field: article, Fold option 2, moving hero, upgrades hero, AppRU mobile.
- Neon violet light bars and streaks: Fold, Elbit, tv+.
- Glowing ring podiums under products: Fold, Elbit inner, tv+.
- Light-wave ribbons: Energy HOMEPAGE, upgrades hero.
- Soft color glows: a #bc32ce@30% rectangle behind the text, a #2e1c9e color grade.
- Slanted portrait strip: AppRU.
- Circular avatar badges with micro captions: Elbit.
- No ribbons or sale badges were seen. The only badge-like items are the "BUY & TRY" lockup and a hidden nPerf badge.

**Image and text separation**
- One of three methods:
  - a flat or gradient purple panel with a curved edge;
  - a dark ground where the photo glows and fades into black (Energy, Elbit, tv+);
  - a full-bleed photo with a transparent→black gradient on the text side (HOMEPAGE).
- Text never sits directly on a busy area of the photo.

**CTAs**
- A white pill with a purple Regular 20 label: 240x50 r33 or 230x50 r25 on desktop, 178x40 on mobile.
- On light backgrounds: a purple gradient pill with white SemiBold text (AppRU, lead form).
- Labels are single infinitive nouns: "לרכישה", "להצטרפות", "שלח"; Russian "Приобрести", "КУПИТЬ …".
- Most 1920x335 heroes have no CTA. CTAs appear on the 1920x500 homepage formats and on cards.

**Copy patterns (Hebrew)**
- Eyebrow is an exclusivity frame: "רק בסלקום…", "חדש בסלקום".
- Headlines address the reader in plural imperative ("הצטרפו… ותחסכו…") or state a benefit ("משדרגים מהירות בקליק", "30 ימי ניסיון").
- A reassurance sub-line: "בלי לחכות לנציג", "בראש שקט", "משתלמות במיוחד".
- Ends with "!" on promotional lines.
- Mixed Hebrew + Latin brand names inline: "Energy", "Galaxy Z Fold8", "tv+", "1Gb".

**Line breaks**
- Desktop headlines break into 2–3 balanced lines by meaning, e.g. "עם הנחה בשעות / שאתם באמת צריכים / וגם בסופ"ש!".
- Mobile re-breaks independently. Eyebrows that are one line on desktop become 2 lines; headlines go from 3 lines to 2.
- Sub-lines are 2 short lines: "המדריך המלא / לבחירת…".

**Legal text**
- A single line starting with "*", Regular 18/16 #fff, directly under the CTA, right-aligned to the text column ("*בכפוף לתקנון Buy&Try").
- Page-level disclaimers are Regular 14 grey #423f45, centered.

**Sub-brands**
- **Cellcom Energy**: dark purple/black night scenes with warm light, Energy yellow #f3fe7e headline accents, the brand written in Latin "Energy".
- **Elbit**: dark navy, a gold gradient headline, a co-brand lockup (Elbit | Cellcom) above the text, and a fixed two-tier formula "<product> / משתלמות במיוחד".
- **AppRU**: Russian LTR left-aligned text inside the Hebrew right-side slot, ALL-CAPS subs and CTAs, cinematic dark navy and red-black cards with r24, the APP.RU logo with "твой кинотеатр", and the purple gradient price "49.90".

## 4. Inconsistencies and surprises
- Text right edges are not consistent: 1600 / 1591 / 1564 / 1476 / 1442 / 1475. Only some respect the 620 px max-width box from 798:1870; Energy is 743, HOMEPAGE 864.
- Fold headline: ExtraBold 128 in a text box only 61 px tall, so the line height is set far below the font size.
- Mobile weight drifts from desktop. Elbit's sub goes from SemiBold on desktop to ExtraBold on mobile. The Energy 2-line variant is SemiBold on desktop and ExtraBold on mobile.
- Several "final" frames ship without live text, with the text layers hidden: article 660:873, tv+ 1266:853 / 1160:29, FOX HOME tiles. This suggests the text is rendered in code over a background image. 1266:853 also holds hidden leftover text from another campaign.
- AppRU 942:1099: the CTA buttons exist but are at opacity 0. The Russian text is LTR-left inside an RTL site layout.
- The Samsung model name uses Samsung's own font at 0.75× the Hebrew size in the same line.
- Two CTA pill radii are in use: 33 and 25.
- Energy mobile 632:415 contains an nPerf "best mobile browsing 2025" badge layer with non-integer font sizes (6.88 / 12.04 / 13.76) that does not render.
